Know what the numbers mean.

Probability is conditional

Monte Carlo uses a constant-volatility geometric Brownian motion, your expected price return, and seed 42. Brownian-bridge sampling estimates between-step target crossings. Touching a target does not mean an option is profitable.

A pricing approximation

Black–Scholes–Merton prices European-style exercise. Standard US stock and ETF calls typically allow early exercise, which this model does not value. It uses a continuous dividend yield, fixed rates and no jumps; earnings, discrete dividends and volatility changes can materially change results.

Risk, not a promise

95% VaR is the 95th percentile of modeled loss; CVaR averages the worst 5%. Neither is a maximum loss. Reward/risk is target-scenario profit divided by the full premium plus entered fees, not a stop-loss ratio. Horizon profit probability assumes a theoretical exit, not a guaranteed fill.
